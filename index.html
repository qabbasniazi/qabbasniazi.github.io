PROFILE

About

Dr. Qaiser Abbas is an academic and researcher in Computer Science, with a PhD in Computational Linguistics/Natural Language Processing from the University of Konstanz, Germany. His work spans natural language processing, computational linguistics, artificial intelligence, information retrieval, machine learning, and algorithmic and probabilistic modeling.
